# LedgerPilot P3 implementation plan

## AI feature expansion — planned 25 November 2026

The [ten-feature AI implementation plan](AI_Features_Implementation_Plan.md) adds AI-01–AI-10 with delivery waves W0–W6, required resources, accounting decisions and passing-test gates. It extends existing P3 packages without renaming them: reconciliation detective, impact preview, closed-loop clarification, approved business memory, continuous close, multilingual voice, contract verification, cash scenarios, portfolio planning and verifiable evidence. These additions are planned, not completion claims. Start with W0 real model/tool execution and evaluation; reuse existing ledger and approval paths.

The [consolidated P3 plan](Next_Phases_Implementation_Plan.md#p3-broader-automation-and-integrations) also lists the SRS accounting, integration and regional access packages P3-I through P3-K.

P3 extends the supported P2 product with approved integrations, richer human-reviewed workflows, controlled payment execution and evidence-backed AI. P3 begins only after P2 production controls, provider eligibility, jurisdiction rules, accounting/security owners and funded operating capacity are established. This is a proposed delivery sequence; estimate and revalidate scope at P3 entry.

## Entry gate

P2 production foundation and recovery are accepted; target jurisdictions and record schedules are approved; provider contracts/sandboxes and privacy/security reviews are complete; P2 document, quality, reconciliation-time and AI-cost baselines are recorded. Human payment execution requires a separately approved payment provider and operating policy.

## Implementation sequence

| Package | Build | Acceptance gate |
|---|---|---|
| **P3-A · Authorized supplier portal connectors** | Build a connector framework and prioritized vendor adapters that fetch invoice/evidence only with authorized accounts. Isolate credentials, support MFA where provider allows, record provenance, handle changing portals, rate limits and manual upload fallback. | Per-vendor terms/security approval, isolated credential tests, auditability, duplicate/replay safety, revocation, and no unauthorized access. Human review remains required before accounting use. |
| **P3-B · Multichannel evidence chasing** | Add consent-based WhatsApp and Slack adapters, with configurable email fallback. Define recipient preferences, sequence, attempt caps, quiet hours, locale/templates, opt-out, delivery status and payment/evidence-state recheck before each send. | Provider sandbox and consent evidence; no message after payment, dispute, opt-out or evidence receipt; retries idempotent; delivery/bounce/complaint behavior and audit verified. |
| **P3-C · Voice-note review** | Accept audio within a declared format/language/duration matrix; transcribe and extract proposed accounting instructions into a review draft linked to the source audio and transcript. | Versioned speech evaluation and confidence/abstention tests; tenant privacy/retention controls; human correction/approval required; no journal posting directly from audio or transcription. |
| **P3-D · Human-approved supplier payment execution** | Integrate one approved payment provider at a time. Verify beneficiary changes out-of-band; require explicit authorized human confirmation, MFA/step-up, limits and dual approval for configured risk; use idempotency, immutable audit, state reconciliation and emergency kill switch. | Provider sandbox and controlled live pilot; duplicate/replay/failure/refund/reversal tests; beneficiary-change and account-takeover scenarios; accounting tie-out; legal/compliance/security approval. AI may propose payment preparation but may never approve or execute it. |
| **P3-E · Accountant portfolio exception workspace** | Build cross-business exception triage, aging, risk/status filters, assignment and drill-through for accountant portfolios; preserve strict per-business permissions and audit. | Tenant isolation tests for every portfolio action; pagination and query budgets; load test at an agreed hundreds-of-businesses cohort; accountant usability acceptance and delegated-access expiry. |
| **P3-F · Expanded AI and evidence-backed benchmarks** | Expand the tested document support matrix; extend cited assistant scenarios through safe report/query tools; publish reviewed OCR, matching, forecast and anomaly benchmarks. Establish quality-adjusted AI cost optimization with a 50% reduction target within six months of the P2 baseline. | Reproducible versioned datasets, subgroup/error and confidence analysis, citation correctness, authorization, abstention and human review. Public benchmark methodology reviewed. Cost target passes only while agreed quality/safety metrics remain within thresholds; otherwise document variance and revise target with approval. |
| **P3-G · Privacy lifecycle and retention automation** | Automate jurisdiction-approved retention, legal holds, export/deletion workflows and tenant envelope-key lifecycle, including provable key destruction where technically supported. Respect backup expiry and legal exceptions. | Independent tenant deletion/key destruction verification; retention and legal-hold tests by record class; restore/backup behavior documented; no representation of deletion beyond the verified cryptographic boundary. |
| **P3-H · One-day reconciliation service target** | Turn the P2 measurement into a scoped operational target for eligible customers, data-complete books and defined exception classes; provide readiness tracking and exception escalation. | Cohort evidence establishes the target and exclusions. Measure completion distribution and failures over an agreed observation period before publication; do not guarantee outcomes for incomplete or exception-heavy books. |

P3 does not introduce autonomous accounting or AI-authorized payments. Human approval is mandatory for payment execution and financial posting. Expand support only within declared provider, language, file and jurisdiction matrices.

## Release gate

Accounting, security, privacy/legal, operations and product owners approve each provider and workflow. The release candidate passes deployed integration, recovery, tenant isolation, payment safety, audit, accessibility and measured capacity checks; support/on-call and rollback procedures are staffed.

See [feature-by-feature delivery plan](P3_P4_Feature_Implementation_Plan.md) for the full implementation order, dependencies and acceptance checks.

### Local implementation record — 2026-11-19

- **Portfolio:** Enable “Show portfolio review status” in the business chooser to review five authorized businesses per page. Each card shows bank rows awaiting review, oldest pending transaction, latest statement date, bills without linked evidence, and prepared close tasks awaiting review after period end. Open the business to act. Portfolio-wide sorting, assignment and capacity verification remain open.
- **Accounting questions:** Reports → Ask about your books answers supported questions about monthly net profit, the largest outstanding customer balance, and monthly expense changes. Answers link to saved tenant-scoped reports. Unsupported questions return guidance. No paid model or external data is used.
- **Journal preparation:** General ledger → Prepare an accounting entry previews accruals, straight-line depreciation installments and FX conversions before explicit posting. Conversion uses a user-supplied rate and source; supported transaction currencies are USD, PKR, GBP, AED and EUR. The original amount and rate are retained in the journal memo. There is no automatic FX feed, revaluation or fixed-asset register in this increment.
- **Local evidence:** PostgreSQL integration covers the three question types, report retrieval, portfolio permissions, all three journal calculations and posting retries. The Chromium acceptance flow checks portfolio status, a cited answer and an approved GBP journal after recording a partial invoice payment. These tests use synthetic books; they do not establish accounting-policy or production sign-off.
Reports now resolves explicit period follow-ups against the last question with a cited answer in the current business. Context is cleared when the business changes.

### P3 accountant workflow update — local verification

- **Portfolio:** `GET /api/v1/portfolio` ranks the full active owner/accountant portfolio before pagination. Filters: minimum exceptions, clear/open status, severity and assignee. Severity currently means pending bank review (1), bill missing evidence (2), or past-period close review (3). Assignment is an append-only event with actor, prior and next accountant, timestamp, tenant checks and stale-write rejection. The web chooser supports sorting, status filtering and assignment. The history API supports paging; the UI currently shows the most recent 100 events.
- **Conversation:** “Which accounts drove that?” ranks signed contributions by absolute change, retaining the preceding quarter comparison when relevant. Expense increases reduce net-profit contribution. “Top 5 expense categories” still ranks raw amounts. The local live Chromium test verified a business switch, unbacked answer, failed request and subsequent follow-up.
- **Accounting:** migration 0036 adds a tenant-scoped asset register, append-only FX quotes and stored period-end proposals. Asset schedules use monthly straight-line only, beginning in the acquisition month and ending before any month end after disposal. Historical dated quotes are cached by currency pair/business/day; explicit overrides take precedence. Optional operator setting `FX_PROVIDER_URL` must identify an HTTPS endpoint accepting `day`, `base` (foreign), `quote` (functional) and returning matching fields plus decimal `rate`. Unavailable or mismatched quotes fail closed. No provider is enabled by default. FX inputs include documented carrying balance and foreign units; these are supplied by the accountant, not inferred from the ledger. Adjustments and installments require the existing journal approval route. Posted journals use the existing reversal workflow.
- **Measured portfolio check:** in backend, set `RUN_PHASE2_INTEGRATION=1`, then run `python -m pytest -qs tests/test_p3_portfolio_scale.py`. An isolated PostgreSQL database held 500 authorized businesses, using generated exceptions as close tasks (not a replay of all generated journals). First / indexed run, milliseconds: sort 7633.8 / 4768.5; next page 7198.2 / 4480.7; clear filter 7667.4 / 4667.7; assignment 1788.7 / 1975.1; assignee filter 2919.4 / 4619.8. These are single HTTP samples under concurrent local testing, not percentiles or capacity sign-off.
- **Open acceptance:** full final regression results, representative ledger-backed performance under controlled load, dedicated reassignment/role-denial coverage, full history paging in the web UI, and final browser coverage of all financial actions. No deployment or production approval inferred.

### P3 Features C, D, and E implementation and verification (24 November 2026)

- **C (`P3-B` Multichannel WhatsApp & Slack reminders):** migration `0039_p3_reminders_portals_payments.py` adds RLS-protected `app.channel_reminder_preferences` and `app.channel_reminders`. Endpoints under `/api/v1/businesses/{business_id}/p3/reminders` manage per-recipient consent (`whatsapp_opt_in`, `slack_opt_in`, `email_fallback`), channel priority, quiet-hour windows, attempt caps, and opt-out. Every dispatch rechecks invoice/bill settlement and evidence linkage (`skipped_resolved`) and enforces idempotency before dispatching via Official Meta WhatsApp Cloud API (`WHATSAPP_PHONE_NUMBER_ID`, `WHATSAPP_ACCESS_TOKEN`), Slack Incoming Webhook / Bot API (`SLACK_WEBHOOK_URL`, `SLACK_BOT_TOKEN` -> `#all-balanc`), or SMTP email fallback.
- **D (`P3-A` Authorized supplier portal invoice connectors):** migration `0039` adds RLS-protected `app.supplier_portal_connections` and `app.supplier_portal_ingestions`. Supports Method A (`api_stripe` vendor invoice fetcher) and Method B (Web Login portals for Utilities, Telecom, and Cloud/Software using a read-only `"Viewer"` sub-user and a **32-character Base32 TOTP seed**). Sub-user passwords and 32-character TOTP seeds are encrypted at rest with `Fernet(evidence_encryption_key)`. During sync, the runner calculates the live 6-digit RFC 6238 TOTP code in memory, enforces SHA-256 content and external-ID duplicate guards (`duplicate_skipped`), halts cleanly on changed portal layouts (`portal_changed`), supports immediate credential revocation, and routes extracted invoices into human-reviewable Purchase Bill Drafts (`ingested_to_draft`) without posting directly to the general ledger.
- **E (`P3-D` Human-approved supplier payment execution):** migration `0039` adds RLS-protected `app.supplier_payment_controls`, `app.supplier_beneficiaries`, and `app.supplier_payment_executions`. Enforces out-of-band beneficiary bank verification (`out_of_band_verified`), automatic invalidation of pending payment drafts whenever beneficiary bank coordinates change (`invalidated`), strict `owner`/`accountant` role authorization, Step-Up 2FA verification, dual approval above `dual_approval_threshold`, and an emergency workspace payment kill switch (`kill_switch_active`). Supports live Stripe Payment Rail execution (`STRIPE_SECRET_KEY` -> `pi_...`) alongside built-in NACHA ACH and SWIFT MT103 wire remittance advice generation.
